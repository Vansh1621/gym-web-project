About us

Built for people who show up.

D Lion Gym opened with one idea: a gym should be minimal, honest, and genuinely helpful — not another place selling you a fancy app you'll never open.

Our story

D Lion Gym is a fitness centre in Yeshwant Colony, Indore, built around well-maintained equipment, a clean and hygienic space, and trainers who are actually experienced. We keep things simple: strength training, cardio, Zumba, and dance fitness, all on one spacious floor.

We're rated 4.5 out of 5 by our members on Google — and we'd rather earn that with consistency than with gimmicks.

What matters to us

  • Real coaching, not just a card swipe.
  • Clean facility, maintained equipment, no long waits.
  • Clear plans and pricing — you always know where you stand.
  • A community that pushes you on the days you don't feel like it.
